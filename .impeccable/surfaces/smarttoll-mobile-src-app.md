# Surface brief: SmartToll app shell and screens (with the website brought in line)

**Mode:** Operate. Motorists plan trips, check RFID balances and follow trips; admins maintain rates on the website.
**Scope:** visual redesign of the whole app and website, applied through the shared theme, components and stylesheet. Behaviour, copy, navigation model and data stay as they are. The Trip in Progress screen keeps its bold, high-contrast driving information.
**Pinned by the user:** "Option B": the reference travel app's shapes, spacing and softness (very rounded airy cards, pill buttons, label-over-value fields, dotted route lines, lighter and larger titles), in SmartToll's own colours (sign green, lane amber) with green-tinted pastel washes instead of lavender. No vouchers, points, ratings, illustrations or tilted cards.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A soft, airy route planner whose every trip reads as a route line: origin, a dotted road, destination. It refuses both the old flat, bordered, uppercase-signage admin look and the lavender consumer-travel template it borrows its softness from.

OWN-WORLD: Pale mint-grey ground, white borderless cards with 24 px corners and a soft green-tinted shadow; near-black overhead-green pill buttons, amber pill for the one "go" action; tinted borderless fields with the label inside; pastel green, amber and sky washes for tiles and badges. Manrope throughout (medium, sentence-case, large titles; heavy figures); Barlow Condensed only for driving signage on the trip screen.

STORY: The motorist sees their trips, balances and next action at a glance, plans with fields that read like a ticket, and saves knowing the toll; on the road, the trip screen stays a high-contrast gantry sign.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home on a pale ground: light header with the SmartToll mark, "Hi, Juan" large and medium-weight, two pill-shaped actions, four pastel figure tiles, then "Recent trips" cards each drawn as origin ⋯ dotted line with time ⋯ destination and the toll in a dark pill. Tab bar white with an active pill behind the icon and the raised amber Plan button.
